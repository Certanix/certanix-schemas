About the regulation references. This evidence pack records tests that Certanix ran and signed. It lists the EU legal provisions those tests relate to, and for each one what the pack does not show. It is not a declaration of conformity, a certification, or a conformity assessment under any EU legislation. It does not replace the manufacturer's own obligations, or the involvement of a notified body where the law requires one. The Certanix signature attests that this record is complete and unchanged since signing. It does not attest that the tested product complies with any requirement.

Each anchor names one clause and the EUR-Lex text it was read from. The clause text itself is not restated here: read it on EUR-Lex. Each entry's legal citation in a pack stands on its own.
